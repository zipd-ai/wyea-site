About WYEA

The engineers who answer your calls

WYEA, Whittle and Ye Engineering Associates, is a principal-led engineering firm in Newport Beach that builds firm-owned document engines for specialty insurers, MGAs and program administrators. Both founders work every engagement, from the first meeting to the last release.
