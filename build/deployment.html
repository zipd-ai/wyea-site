WYEA / What we build / Deployment and control

What we build · 04

Your firm's environment, your firm's audit trail

Everything WYEA builds runs in an environment your firm controls: a single-tenant deployment with firm-held keys, an append-only record of every document access, and retention rules your firm sets.

Book a 30-minute call Read the security posture

How it is built

Three deployments, one standard

The isolation, the logging, and the training guarantee are identical in all three. Only the hosting changes.

Where it runs

The choice is about who holds the infrastructure, and it is made by your firm before anything is built.

Managed single tenant

We run and maintain a deployment that belongs to your firm alone. No shared index, no shared storage, no pooled traffic.

Your own cloud account

The same system, deployed into infrastructure your firm owns and pays for directly, with our access scoped and logged.

On your premises

For matters that cannot leave the building, the system runs on hardware in your office, with no outbound path for documents.

What the boundary means

Training, analytics, and product improvement all sit outside the boundary, and they stay outside it.

No training egress

Client documents are never sent anywhere to train a model. There is no path in the deployment that would allow it.

No shared index

One firm per deployment. Nothing your firm uploads is searchable from another client's system, because there is no other client in it.

Keys your firm holds

Documents are encrypted at rest under keys your firm controls, and rotating or revoking them is your firm's decision.

What the record answers

Questions the audit trail can settle

It is readable by your firm at any time, without asking us, and it records our access on the same terms as anyone else's.

Who opened this document, and when?

Every read is written to an append-only record with the account, the time, and the address it came from. The record covers documents opened through the application and documents pulled through any connected system.

Did WYEA access this matter?

Support sessions are scoped to a named engineer, limited in time, and logged in the same trail as your own staff, so the answer is in the same place and does not depend on us confirming it.

What was exported, and by whom?

Exports are recorded with the account, the scope of the export, and the destination, which is what a client asking about a leak actually needs to know.

What was deleted, and on whose instruction?

Deletions are recorded as events rather than as absences, so a document that is gone still has a history explaining why it is gone.

Which version of the system produced this?

Releases are recorded against the matter, so work product can be tied to the version of the software that generated it, years later.
