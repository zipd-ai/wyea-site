What we build · 01

First drafts in your firm's format, with every citation checked

Upload what you were served. The system reads it against your matter file, drafts a response in your own pleading format, and checks every citation word for word against the source document before it appears.

How it is built

Two rules the system is organized around

Everything else on this page follows from these.

What it may draw on

The documents your firm supplied are the whole world the draft can reach. Nothing outside them is treated as a source.

The papers you were served

Motions, requests, declarations, and exhibits, read in full, with the points that need a response listed before drafting starts.

Your matter file

Pleadings, transcripts, correspondence, and productions already in the matter, so the draft answers from the record.

Your firm's templates

Caption, numbering, headings, and signature block, so the draft opens in Word looking like your firm's work.

How a citation earns its place

A quoted passage has to be found in a document you supplied before it is allowed to appear in the draft.

Located, not recalled

The passage is searched for in the source document. Nothing is reconstructed from memory of what the document said.

Compared word for word

The candidate quote and the located text are compared character by character, not paraphrase to paraphrase.

Flagged when it is not there

No match means the citation is held for attorney review and marked in the draft. It is never quietly dropped or asserted.

How verification works

Four steps, and a rule that cannot be switched off

Verification is how the system is built, not a setting on a screen somewhere.

  1. 01

    Sources are fixed first

    The documents in the matter file are the only material the draft can draw on.

  2. 02

    Candidates are proposed

    The draft is written with candidate citations attached to the exact passage each one claims to rest on.

  3. 03

    Each quote is matched

    Every candidate quote is searched for in its source document and compared word for word.

  4. 04

    Matched, or flagged

    A match is cited with a pointer to the page and line. Anything else is flagged and held for attorney review.

Worked examples

What firms put through it

Each of these starts from documents the firm already has, and ends in a draft an attorney edits.

Opposition to a motion to compel

The moving papers and the discovery at issue go in. What comes back is a statement of the disputed requests, the responses already served, and a draft opposition in your caption, with every transcript and declaration quote located in the source before it is cited.

Responses to written discovery

Requests are broken out one by one against the matter file, with a draft response and the objections your firm actually uses. Anything the record does not support is left blank and flagged rather than filled in.

Demurrer and motion to strike

The complaint is read paragraph by paragraph against the elements of each cause of action, and the draft points to the specific paragraphs at issue rather than to the pleading as a whole.

Deposition summary with citations

A transcript comes back as a summary where every line rests on a page-and-line pointer, so the summary can be checked against the transcript in seconds.

Client status letter from the matter file

Recent filings, orders, and deadlines in the matter are assembled into a letter in your firm's format, with each statement traceable to the document it came from.
