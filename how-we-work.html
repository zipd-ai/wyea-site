How we work

One week to a working prototype, then a fixed price for the build

We build document engines for specialty insurers, MGAs and law firms, and every engagement starts the same way. A call, which is either a consultation or a demo on your own documents, then an NDA, then something your people can try on live work inside the first week. Nothing larger is quoted until your firm has used something real.

How the engagement is shaped

Three commitments that decide everything else

The way the work is priced and staffed is the reason the rest of it holds.

Priced before it starts

Your firm knows the number before any work begins, at every stage, including the first one.

Fixed price

A price for the work, agreed in advance. There is no open-ended hourly bill for exploratory work.

Per engagement

One price for the system you asked for. Hiring three more people does not change it.

Scoped in writing

Deliverables and dates are written down and signed before any build work begins.

Staffed by the people who build it

The firm is two engineers, and both are on your engagement from the first meeting to the last release.

No handoffs

Nobody who was not in the room when your workflow was explained will be the one writing the code for it.

No support tier

When something breaks you write to the person who wrote it.

Training by the builders

Sessions run in your office by the engineers, repeated for new staff without a separate charge.

Owned by you

What is delivered is a system you hold, and the exit terms are settled before anything is built.

Your data, outright

Your documents, and what the system has learned from them, belong to you. Today we return or destroy them on your instruction and confirm it in writing.

Nobody else in it

Your system has its own database, network and sign-in. Nothing is pooled with another client.

Terms up front

Code ownership and licensing are set in the engagement agreement, with the options in front of you first.

What it costs

Every price is quoted before the work starts

Pricing is per engagement, so it does not change when you hire.

Every engagement starts with a one-week prototype on your own documents, at no charge. If the prototype earns it, we quote the full build at a fixed price, with deliverables and dates in writing. Builds start at $25,000. Once the system is live, the monthly fee starts at $5,500 and covers hosting, support and every model provider cost.

Questions clients ask

Before the first call

How long does the whole thing take?

A working prototype is in your team's hands in the first week. A full build is scoped from there, and the scope is written down with dates before work starts, so the answer stops being an estimate.

What if the prototype does not convince us?

Then the engagement ends there, at no cost to you, and your documents are returned or destroyed on your instruction. That is why the prototype comes first.

Who trains our staff?

The two engineers who wrote the software. Training is part of the engagement, run in your office, and repeated for new staff without a separate charge.

What happens when something breaks?

You call or write to the people who built it. There is no support queue in between. Meet the principals.

Do you work outside legal?

Yes. The platform is built around documents, versions and evidence, and it runs in insurance as well as legal. For specialty insurers.
