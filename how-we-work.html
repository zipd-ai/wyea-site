How we work

One week to a working prototype, then a fixed price for the build

We build firm-owned document engines for specialty insurers, MGAs and program administrators, and every engagement starts the same way: a call, an NDA, then something your team can try on live work inside the first week.

The engagement

Six steps, each priced before it starts

Nothing larger is quoted until your team has used a prototype on live work.

  1. Day 0

    Call

    Thirty minutes, booked as a consultation or a demo, at no charge. We ask which documents take your team the longest.

  2. Day 1

    NDA

    Signed before we are sent a single document. Yours or ours.

  3. Week 1

    Prototype

    Built on your real documents and put in front of the people who will use it. Fixed price, quoted before work starts.

  4. Week 2

    Fixed-price scope

    If the prototype earns it, the build is quoted at a fixed price, with deliverables and dates in writing.

  5. Build

    Build

    Both principals build it, and they are the ones writing the code.

  6. After

    Support

    One system for you alone, run and maintained by the two engineers who wrote it.

How the engagement is shaped

Priced, staffed and owned

The way the work is priced and staffed is the reason the rest of it holds.

Priced before it starts

You know the number before any work begins, at every stage, including the first one.

Fixed price

A price for the work, agreed in advance. There is no open-ended hourly bill for exploratory work.

Per engagement

One price for the system you asked for. Hiring three more underwriters does not change it.

Scoped in writing

Deliverables and dates are written down and signed before any build work begins.

Staffed by the people who build it

The firm is two engineers, and both are on your engagement from the first meeting to the last release.

The same two people

The engineers who hear how your wordings team works are the ones who write the code for it.

Direct support

When something breaks you write to the person who wrote it.

Training by the builders

Sessions are run by the engineers and repeated for new staff without a separate charge.

Owned by you

What the system learns from your documents belongs to you, and the terms are settled before anything is built.

Your data, outright

Your documents, and what the system has learned from them, belong to you. Today we return or destroy them on your instruction and confirm it in writing.

Your own system

It has its own database, network and sign-in. Nothing is pooled with another client.

Terms up front

The terms for taking it with you are written into the agreement before the build starts.

Questions

Before the first call

How long does it take?

A working prototype is in your team's hands in the first week. The full build is scoped from there, and the scope is written down with dates before work starts.

What if the prototype does not convince us?

Then the engagement ends there. You have paid the prototype's fixed price and nothing more, and your documents are returned or destroyed on your instruction.

Who trains our staff?

The two engineers who wrote the software. Training is part of the engagement and is repeated for new staff without a separate charge.

What happens when something breaks?

You call or write to the people who built it. There is no support queue in between. Meet the principals.

How are multi-entity groups priced?

Multi-entity groups are quoted at a fixed price after the prototype, once we have seen how many entities, forms and markets the build has to cover.
