Questions partners ask

The details, before you have to ask

How do you handle privilege and confidentiality?

We sign an NDA before seeing a single document. Client materials live in your firm's isolated environment, are never used to train any model, and every access is logged in a tamper-evident audit trail you can inspect. On-premise deployment is available when a matter demands it.

What does an engagement cost?

Every engagement starts with a fixed-price four-week prototype, quoted before we begin — you'll never see an open-ended hourly bill for exploratory work. If the prototype earns it, we scope the full build the same way: fixed price, defined deliverables.

How is this different from Harvey, CoCounsel, or other legal AI platforms?

Those are subscriptions to someone else's product; your firm adapts to them. We build one system around your documents, your local rules, and your workflow — and the engineers who built it answer your phone calls. For many firms the platforms are the right call; a first conversation will tell you honestly which you need.

What happens if the AI can't verify a citation?

It is flagged and held for attorney review — never asserted. A citation appears in output only when its exact quote is found in the source document. That rule is architectural, not a setting.

Do we own the software?

Your firm owns its data, its documents, and its deployment outright. Code ownership and licensing are set in the engagement agreement — we'll put the options in front of you before anything is built.
