How it works

Inside the work

The software does the reading. Your attorneys keep the judgment.

Where the line falls is a design decision, and it is the same on every engagement.

What the software does

The mechanical part of a matter: reading, locating, counting, and formatting, done the same way every time.

Reads what was served

Motions, requests, declarations, and exhibits are read against the matter file, and the points that need a response are listed.

Locates every quoted passage

Each candidate quote is searched for in its source document and compared word for word, not paraphrase to paraphrase.

Counts the days

Court holidays, weekends, and service-method extensions applied, with the governing rule shown next to the date.

What an attorney decides

Every call that carries a consequence stays with a person at your firm, and is recorded with their name.

Whether a citation stands

Unmatched citations are flagged and held for review. The software never asserts one on its own.

Whether a document is privileged

Candidates are surfaced with the reason they were raised. The call is an attorney's, every time.

Whether anything is filed

Nothing leaves the firm without a person releasing it, and the release is recorded.

What your firm keeps

The system is a deliverable your firm holds, not access your firm rents.

The deployment

Single tenant, in an environment your firm controls, cloud or on your own hardware.

The audit trail

Append only, readable by your firm at any time, covering our access on the same terms as yours.

The data

Your documents and your matter record, exported in an open format whenever you ask for them.

Questions partners ask

The details, before you have to ask

Who will we actually work with?

Both of the firm's principal engineers, directly. Your questions go to the person who wrote the code, not into a support queue, so answers come back in minutes, and nothing is lost in relay. Meet the principals.

How do you handle privilege and confidentiality?

We sign an NDA before seeing a single document. Client materials live in your firm's isolated environment, are never used to train any model, and every access is logged in a tamper-evident audit trail you can inspect. On-premise deployment is available when a matter demands it. Read the full security posture.

What does an engagement cost?

Every engagement starts with a fixed-price one-week prototype, quoted before we begin. You'll never see an open-ended hourly bill for exploratory work. If the prototype earns it, we scope the full build the same way: fixed price, defined deliverables. See the engagement model.

How is this different from Harvey, CoCounsel, or the other platforms?

Those are subscriptions to someone else's product; your firm adapts to them. We build one system around your documents, your local rules, and your workflow, and the engineers who built it answer your phone calls. For many firms the platforms are the right call; a first conversation will tell you honestly which you need. Compare the three paths.

What happens when a citation can't be verified?

It is flagged and held for attorney review, never asserted. A citation appears in output only when its exact quote is found in the source document. That rule is architectural, not a setting. How verification works.

Do we own the software?

Your firm owns its data, its documents, and its deployment outright. Code ownership and licensing are set in the engagement agreement. We'll put the options in front of you before anything is built.
