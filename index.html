Questions

What is policy and claims automation for specialty insurers?

It is software that does the routine document work in policy and claims: it reads policies with the endorsements that amend them, checks claims against cover, and runs drafts and checks on its own while your reviewer signs off. WYEA builds one engine for each insurer, from the insurer's own documents.

Who is it for?

Specialty and E&S carriers, fronting carriers, MGAs and program administrators, and reinsurers, intermediaries and run-off teams.

How do you work with our team?

We deploy a team of engineers to your site. They learn your documents and your problems first-hand, and the same team builds and supports your system.

Does it make claims or underwriting decisions?

No. It finds the wording that applies, drafts and flags what it could not read. It never binds or sends. Your reviewer signs off, and your adjuster or underwriter decides.

Where does it read our documents?

Where you keep them, including Dropbox, SharePoint, OneDrive, Google Drive, iManage, NetDocuments and Outlook. Your own system stays the record.

Do our documents train a model?

No. Each insurer has its own isolated system, with its own database, network and sign-in, and client documents never train a model.

What is the investment to start?

We build a prototype on your own documents at a fixed investment agreed before work starts. Builds are from $30,000, and the monthly investment is from $7,500 and covers hosting, support and model usage.
