What we build

Built around the matters your firm already runs

WYEA builds four kinds of system for law firms. Most engagements start with one, and the rest are added as the practice asks for them. All four are shaped around your documents, your templates, and the rules that govern your matters.

Why a firm has software built

Three reasons partners commission a build

Custom is not the right answer for every firm. These are the cases where it pays for itself.

Capacity without headcount

The reading, indexing, and first-pass drafting that consume associate hours are handled by software, so the same team carries more matters.

Work that matches your practice

Your captions, your templates, your local rules, your intake process. Nothing is bent to fit an assumption someone else's product made.

A system your firm controls

Your data, your environment, your audit trail, and a price that does not change with your headcount.

The four systems

What a firm can have built

Each is a real deliverable with a fixed price, not a module in a subscription.

01  Verified drafting

Upload what you were served. Get a grounded review and a draft response in your firm's own pleading format.

Your format, not ours

Caption, numbering, headings, and signature block taken from the templates your firm already files.

Every citation checked

Each quoted passage is located in the source document and compared word for word before it is cited.

Unmatched held back

Anything that cannot be located is flagged in the draft and held for attorney review, never asserted.

02  Discovery and review

Production sets read, indexed, and ranked against the requests your firm was actually served.

Ranked against your requests

Scoring is tied to the requests served in your matter, with the request number shown on every hit.

Privilege held for an attorney

Candidates are surfaced with the reason they were raised. The call is always a person's.

Log out of the same record

Bates, custodians, and dates carry through, so the privilege log matches the review that produced it.

03  Matter workflow

Deadlines computed from the rules that govern the matter and calendared with the authority attached.

Rules, not guesses

Service method, court, and local rules are carried into every calculation and cited on the entry.

Your lead times too

The internal deadlines your firm works to sit on the same calendar as the court dates.

Conflicts at intake

Parties and related entities checked against existing matters before the file is opened.

04  Deployment and control

The environment the other three run in: single tenant, isolated, audited, and yours.

Hosted your way

A managed single-tenant deployment, your own cloud account, or hardware in your office.

Audited end to end

An append-only record of every document access, readable by your firm at any time.

Yours to keep

Retention and deletion set by your firm, and an export in an open format whenever you ask.

Security and confidentiality

All four run inside your firm's boundary

Confidentiality is the first engineering requirement on every engagement, ahead of any feature.

One firm per deployment

Your matter data stays in an environment your firm controls. Nothing leaves it for training, analytics, or product improvement, and every access is recorded in a log your firm can read.

  • NDA signed before we see a single document
