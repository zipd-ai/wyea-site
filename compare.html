  • Your documents are mostly standard forms
  • You need something running this month
  • A per-seat price matters more than fit

Your own engine fits better when

  • Your wordings are manuscript or heavily endorsed
  • The same deal is papered on several entities' forms
  • Your programs run across several carriers
  • Your house rules are what make your book different

Before you choose

When is a shared product the better choice?

When your documents are mostly standard forms, you need it running this month, or a per-seat price matters more. We will say so on the first call.

Can we start with a shared product and move later?

Yes. Your documents stay in your own document system either way. Ask any vendor how you would take your history with you.

What does a firm-owned engine cost?

Builds from $30,000. Then from $7,500 a month, with hosting, support and model costs included. Multi-entity groups are quoted at a fixed price after the prototype. Cost in detail
