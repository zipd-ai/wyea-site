Questions

Which lines of business is this for?

Any line where the paper is written for the deal. If your documents are mostly standard forms, a shared product will usually serve you better, and we will say so on the first call.

Does the engine make underwriting or claims decisions?

No. It finds, drafts and flags. Your people make every decision.

What will a vendor review ask that you cannot answer yet?

A SOC 2 report or ISO certificate, a penetration test, hosting in your own cloud account, and keys you hold yourself. We have none of those today.

Who owns what gets built?

What the system learns from your documents belongs to you. The terms for taking it with you are written into the agreement before the build starts.

What does it cost?

The prototype is a fixed price, quoted before work starts. Builds from $25,000. Then from $5,500 a month, with hosting, support and model costs included.
