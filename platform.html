WYEA / Platform

Platform

A knowledge system that can show you where every answer came from

The system reads the documents your firm already keeps, answers questions against them, and binds each answer to the exact version of the exact file it was found in. Where it cannot find something, it says so.

Book a 30-minute call How we work

The shape of it

Four parts

Each one exists because of a specific way document systems behave, not because it rounded out a diagram.

Connected sources

Your document store, your mail, and the public record. Your store stays the system of record.

We mirror, we do not own

The platform never becomes a second library your people have to keep in sync. Your store remains the thing that is true.

Providers differ, and it matters

Version durability, digest algorithm, change-feed shape and access control all vary. The connector declares what it can actually promise.

Filenames are not identity

A filename at a firm is often the sensitive part. Path and title never participate in identity or authorization.

Grounding and verification

A quote is located in the source or it does not become an answer.

It is there, or it is flagged

A passage has to be in one of your documents before it is written as an answer. Not found means you see it flagged, not stated.

No confidence score

There is no number between right and wrong for somebody to read generously at six in the evening.

Unreadable is its own answer

If the source could not be opened, the result is not checked. That is different from checked and false.

Evidence and the record

An answer points at the document as your own system had it that day, not as we would like to remember it.

You are not asked to trust our word

What an answer points at is your own system's record of that document, not a certificate we issued ourselves.

Old answers are never rewritten

When a document changes, the answer you were already given keeps pointing at what it was actually based on.

It outlives your provider's memory

Dropbox and the rest drop old versions on a schedule of their own. Work you have already relied on stays openable anyway.

Isolation

One firm, one cell. There is no shared content plane to leak across.

Own database, network, identity

The per-firm boundary is a real infrastructure boundary, not a tenant column in a shared table.

Nothing pooled

No shared corpus, no cross-firm retrieval, no training on your content. Instances share code and never data.

We publish the limits

WYEA operates the cell. The security page states what that does and does not mean today.

Where it is used

Two lines of work

The system is built around documents, versions and evidence, so the profession sitting on top of it is configuration rather than architecture.

Legal

Matters, served paper, discovery, motions and deadlines, with the public court record and case law connected alongside the firm's own files. This is where the platform started and where most of the domain work sits today.

Insurance

The same reading problem with different paper. Submissions, policies, endorsements and correspondence, where the question is usually what a specific document actually says rather than what someone remembers it saying.
