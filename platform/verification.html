The three outcomes

Kept apart on purpose

Collapsing the third into the first is where confident nonsense comes from.

Located

The quote was found in the source text, character for character, after whitespace is normalised.

Its place travels with it

The position of the quote in the document is recorded, so a reviewer opens the document and lands on the text.

So does the version

A position means nothing without the version it refers to, so both are stored together.

It stays checkable later

Because the answer points at the document as it read that day, it still opens after somebody edits the file.

Not located

The quote was looked for and was not in the source.

Flagged and kept

Removing it would hide that the system tried and failed, which a reviewer needs to see.

Never softened

There is no path that rewrites an unsupported claim into something blander that escapes the check.

Counted, and shown to you

The reviewer sees how many claims failed as well as the ones that passed.

Not checked

The source could not be read when the answer was produced.

Permissions move

A document readable on Monday can be walled on Tuesday, and the honest result is that nobody checked.

History expires

When a version has aged out of the provider's retention window, the system says so and does not read a different version.

A limitation is a result

Typed limitations come back attached to the document. They do not disappear into a confident answer.

What this costs you

Fewer answers, and that is the point

A system with this rule will decline to answer things a looser one would answer fluently. That is the trade.

It will say it does not know

When your documents do not contain the answer, nothing is produced. You see a gap.

It cannot summarise what it cannot read

A scanned page with no usable text is reported as a limitation. The system does not guess at it.

It does not decide

The platform locates and assembles. Judgment and the decision to bind or send anything stay with your people.
