What holds today

These are properties of the running system today.

No shared content plane

There is no shared pile to search, because there is no shared store of anybody's documents at all.

No training on your content

Not by us and not by a provider underneath us. Instances share code and no data.

Your store stays authoritative

The platform reads from your document system and does not become a competing copy of it.

What we are not claiming

Some of what a security questionnaire asks for is not finished.

We run it

It runs on our infrastructure. It does not run inside your own cloud account or on hardware in your building.

Export and deletion are not built

Offboarding is designed and not yet implemented. Ask, and we will show you the current state honestly.

Support access is not client-visible

It is scoped, but you cannot yet watch it in a log of your own. That gap is real and we are closing it.
