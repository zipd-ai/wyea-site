Custom AI software for law firms in Orange County

Published August 9, 2026 · Reviewed September 2026

WYEA builds custom AI software for law firms in Orange County, California. Instead of subscribing to a platform and adapting your practice to it, your firm gets one system built around your documents, your local rules, and your workflow, with every AI citation checked word-for-word against the source document: verified, or flagged for attorney review.

What are a small California firm's options for legal AI?

A firm evaluating legal AI in 2026 has three realistic paths:

  1. Subscription platforms: Harvey, Thomson Reuters CoCounsel, Spellbook, and similar products. Broad, proven, priced per seat; your firm adapts its workflow to the product.
  2. General AI tools: ChatGPT or Claude with careful prompting. Inexpensive, but nothing is verified against your documents, and confidentiality depends on settings your staff must get right every time.
  3. Custom software: a system built for your firm's documents and practice area, owned and deployed your way. This is what WYEA does, and it fits firms whose workflow doesn't match what the platforms assume.

For many firms the platforms are the right call. A first conversation with us will tell you honestly which path fits. Attorneys shop carefully here:

One in three attorneys who book a legal-tech demo book more within a week, evaluating an average of 3.2 vendors (FlyTech Q2 2026 report, via LawSites).

How is client confidentiality protected?

Client documents never train a model, and every access is auditable. We sign an NDA before seeing anything. Materials live in your firm's isolated environment, cloud or on your own infrastructure, and tamper-evident records show exactly what happened, and when, to every document we touch. Read the full security posture.

Data security is the legal profession's top concern about AI, 46% of legal professionals rank it first, ahead of ethics at 42% (8am 2026 Legal Industry Report). We treat it as the first engineering requirement, not a compliance checkbox.

What happens when the AI can't verify a citation?

It is flagged and held for attorney review, never asserted. A citation appears in output only when its exact quote is found in the source document. That rule is architectural, not a setting, and it is the difference between AI you can put in a filing and AI you have to re-check line by line. See how verification works.

What does custom legal AI software cost?

Every WYEA engagement starts with a fixed-price, one-week prototype built against your firm's real documents, quoted before we begin, never an open-ended hourly bill. If the prototype earns it, the full build is scoped the same way: fixed price, defined deliverables. Your firm owns its data, its documents, and its deployment outright. See the engagement model.

Who builds it?

Two principal engineers, with backgrounds at Amazon, a Fortune 100 bank's corporate-risk machine-learning group, Georgia Tech, and UC San Diego, based in Newport Beach. We come to your office, and the engineers who build your system are the ones who answer your calls. Meet the principals.
