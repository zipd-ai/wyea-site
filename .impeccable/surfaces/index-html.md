# Home surface brief

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: an insurer's underwriting or claims lead deciding whether to book a demo. Direction pinned by the user: look and feel of ode.com (Anderson, 2026-10-20), replacing Direction A. Scope: home first, global tokens, header, footer, buttons; inner pages follow after visual sign-off. Build path: code-first (no image generation available). ## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet, confident page where light is the imagery: soft drifting fields behind large light-weight type, and the product shown as translucent paper sheets that answer with their source. Refuses the dense card grid and the gradient-panel-with-screenshot template.
OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground, warm brown-black ink, light-weight Geist at display scale, dotted hairlines, small tracked uppercase pill buttons with a drawn arrow, blush and periwinkle blurred light fields with fine grain, asymmetric label-left, copy-right sections.
