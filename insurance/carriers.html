Questions

Can it draft the same deal for two of our entities?

Yes. Each draft follows that entity's own forms and house rules, and stays a draft until a reviewer approves it.

Does it read our program business as well as our direct book?

Yes. Program wordings, binding authority agreements and the policies under them are read together.

Does it bind or issue anything?

No. It finds, drafts and flags. Underwriting, binding and issuing stay with your people.
