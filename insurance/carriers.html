Questions

What carriers ask first

Can it draft the same deal for two of our entities?

Yes. The same deal can be drafted on the forms of each carrier entity or market you write through. Each draft follows that entity's own forms and house rules, and stays a draft until a reviewer approves it.

Does it read our program business as well as our direct book?

Yes. Program wordings, binding authority agreements and the policies issued under them are read together, so a question about one program comes back with the agreement and the policies that answer it.

Does it bind or issue anything?

No. The engine finds, drafts and flags. Underwriting, binding and issuing stay with your people.
