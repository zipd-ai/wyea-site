Questions

Can it compare two versions of the same treaty?

Yes. Each answer points at the wording as it read that day. Two renewals can be compared clause by clause.

Can it read older scanned wordings?

Where a page has readable text, yes. A scan with no readable text comes back to your team, unguessed.

Is our book mixed with anyone else's?

No. Each client has its own database, network and sign-in. Nothing you put in trains a model.
