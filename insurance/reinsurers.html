Questions

What reinsurers ask first

Can it compare two versions of the same treaty?

Yes. Each answer points at the wording as it read on the day, so two renewals can be compared clause by clause, each quoted from its own version.

Can it read older scanned wordings?

Where a page has readable text, yes. Where a page is a scan with no readable text, it comes back to your team unanswered, and nothing is guessed.

Is our book mixed with anyone else's?

No. Each client runs in its own system, with its own database, network and sign-in. Nothing is retrieved across clients and nothing you put in trains a model.
