Questions

What mgas ask first

Can it work across more than one carrier's forms?

Yes. Each carrier's forms and binding authority agreement are read alongside your own program wordings, and a draft can be produced on the forms of each carrier you write for.

What happens to our documents if we change carriers?

Nothing moves. Your documents stay in your own document system, and what the engine has learned from them belongs to you. The terms for taking it with you are written into the agreement before the build starts.

Does it make underwriting decisions?

No. It finds, drafts and flags. Underwriting, referrals and binding stay with your people and your carriers' rules.
