# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Underwriting, claims and operations leaders at specialty insurers, MGAs and program administrators (carriers, fronting and E&S carriers, reinsurers and run-off). They review vendors with a security questionnaire before they buy, and they are judged on accuracy, so they read claims skeptically.

## Product Purpose
WYEA builds firm-owned document engines: answers and drafts from the policies, claims files and data an insurer already keeps, with every line showing its source. A reviewer signs off; the engine never binds or sends. Success is a booked demo.

## Positioning
Insights and drafting focused on policy and claims workflow. One system per insurer, answers that keep the version they were based on, and a passage it cannot find never becomes an answer.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Insurance only. Prototype is fixed price, quoted before work starts; builds from $30,000, monthly from $7,500 with hosting and model usage included. Held: SOC 2, ISO 27001, independent penetration test, MFA, encryption in transit and at rest. Never claim customer-managed keys, hosting in a client's cloud or on premises, automated export or deletion, or a client-readable support-access log. Every "Book a demo" goes to /demo.

## Brand Commitments
Voice: plain, concrete, no clever contrast lines, no hype words, no em or en dashes. "Enterprise" names the audience only. WYEA's own figures are an investment, never a cost.

## Evidence on Hand
Principals' photos in assets/. Sourced industry statistics with working links. No customers, logos, testimonials, metrics or case studies exist; every example is invented and labeled invented.

## Product Principles
1. Show the source: proof is the product, so figures and examples display their provenance.
2. Never name a client; examples are invented and labeled.
3. Say what it does, not what it feels like.
4. The reviewer stays in charge; the design never implies autonomy it does not have.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (checked by npm run contrast); motion respects prefers-reduced-motion; content is visible without scripting.
