What security teams ask

Do you train on our documents?

No. Nothing you put in trains a model, ours or a provider's. The agreement says so too.

Where does our content live?

Your document system stays the system of record. We run your system on our infrastructure, not in your cloud account.

Can we see an audit trail?

Events go to an append-only record. You cannot open a view of it yet.

What happens if we stop working with you?

Offboarding is manual today. We return or destroy content on your instruction and confirm it in writing.

Do you have SOC 2?

No. We hold no SOC 2 report, ISO certificate or penetration test. If you need one today, we are not the right vendor yet.
