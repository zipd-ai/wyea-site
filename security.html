What we do not have today

Each item below is something a vendor review may ask for. Ask for the current position in writing and you will get it.

  • SOC 2 report, ISO 27001 certificate

    There is no report or certificate to hand you. When that changes, this page will say so and name the auditor.

    Not held
  • Independent penetration test

    No third-party test has been run.

    Not held
  • Hosting in your own cloud or on premises

    Each system runs on our infrastructure. Running it inside your cloud account or on your hardware is designed and not built.

    Not built
  • Customer-managed keys

    Content is encrypted at rest by the platform. Keys you hold and can revoke independently of us are not offered.

    Not built
  • Automated export and deletion

    Retention, export, deletion and offboarding are specified and not implemented. Until they are, we run offboarding with you by hand and confirm it in writing.

    Not built
  • A log of our support access that you can read

    Our support access is scoped and limited. You cannot yet see it in a log of your own.

    Not built

Questions

What security teams ask

Do you train on our documents?

No. Nothing you put in trains a model, ours or a model provider's underneath us. This goes in the engagement agreement as well as on this page.

Where does our content live?

Your own document system stays the system of record. We run one system for you, with its own database, network and sign-in, on our infrastructure. It does not run inside your cloud account or on your hardware today.

When do you sign an NDA?

Before we are sent a single document. We prefer your form. If you would rather not draft one, ours takes about five minutes to read.

Can we see an audit trail?

Events are written to an append-only record that is protected against edit and deletion at the database level. A view of it that you can open yourself, including of our own support access, does not exist yet. We will show you the current state on a call.

What happens to our content if we stop working with you?

Today offboarding is manual: we return or destroy content on your instruction and confirm it in writing. If a contractual deadline for return matters to you, tell us early and we will write the manual process into the agreement.

Do you have SOC 2?

No. There is no SOC 2 report, no ISO certificate and no third-party penetration test. If a certificate is a hard requirement for you, we are not the right vendor yet, and we will say so on the first call.
