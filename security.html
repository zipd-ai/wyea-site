Security and confidentiality

Your clients' documents never train a model, and you can audit everything

Confidentiality is the first engineering requirement on every engagement, ahead of any feature. These are commitments in the engagement agreement, enforced by how the system is built.

Talk to us about your obligations Deployment options

Six commitments

What your firm is promised, in writing

Each of these is a term in the engagement agreement and a constraint in the build, not a preference we hold.

Before we see a document

The protections that are in place before your firm sends us anything at all.

NDA first

Signed before we are sent a single document. Your firm's own form is preferred; ours is available if you would rather not draft one.

One firm per deployment

Single tenant, with no shared index and no shared storage. Nothing your firm uploads is pooled with another client's matter.

No model training, ever

Client documents are never used to train a model, by us or by anyone else, and the deployment has no outbound path that would allow it.

While we are working

What your firm can see and control for as long as the system is running.

Audit trail you can read

Every document access is written to an append-only record your firm can inspect at any time, without asking us first.

Our access is scoped

Support sessions are limited in time and scope, tied to a named engineer, and logged in the same trail as anyone else's.

Deletion on your instruction

Retention is set by your firm. When you ask for documents to go, they go, and the deletion is recorded as an event.

Deployment options

Questions your general counsel will ask

Answered plainly

Do you train on our documents?

No. Client documents are never used to train any model, by us or by anyone else. The guarantee is written into the engagement agreement, and the deployment has no outbound path that would allow it.

Where does our data actually live?

In a single-tenant environment your firm controls: a managed deployment that belongs to your firm alone, your own cloud account, or hardware in your office. There is no shared index, and nothing is pooled across clients.

Can we see what you accessed?

Yes. Every document access is recorded in an append-only audit trail your firm can read at any time without asking us. Our own support sessions are scoped, time-limited, and recorded on the same terms.

When do you sign an NDA?

Before we see a single document. We prefer your firm's own form. If you would rather not draft one, ours is ready and takes a partner about five minutes to read.

What happens to our documents if we stop working with you?

Your data is exported in an open format and the deployment is handed over or destroyed, on your instruction. The exit terms are part of the engagement agreement, agreed before any build begins.

Can this run entirely inside our office?

Yes. On-premise deployment is available when a matter demands it, with no outbound path for documents. The isolation, logging, and training guarantees are the same as in every other deployment.

How do you handle a security incident?

You hear from a principal, not a status page. Notification terms, including timing, are set in the engagement agreement before any documents change hands, and the audit trail is yours to inspect throughout.
